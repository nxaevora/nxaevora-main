The candidate neutralizes that specific color while retaining its linear luminance and alpha/strength. It only matches the two known lighting shaders and the exact captured pair of category1 color entries. Other materials in that same category and matching lighting setup may also change. It does not target the box by object identity. Visual results are unverified.

F3 diagnostics remain enabled; constant snapshots are labeled as CPU copies with the enabled rim transform.

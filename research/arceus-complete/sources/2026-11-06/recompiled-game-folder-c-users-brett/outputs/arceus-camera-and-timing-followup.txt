NEW RESEARCH FINDINGS

1. Two additional blink bodies in ride-controller code:
   main+2518BA8 (size FC) is BYTE-IDENTICAL to main+194F110.
   main+2518CA4 (size FC) implements the +48 base-subobject variant.
   Both decrement +68C and use virtual slots +358/+360/+368/+370.
   The primary has direct callers 2549FA0, 256C3AC, 256C478, 25A0534, 25A12EC.
   Its vtables and constructors lead to ride controller setup, including
   nyuura/climbing and other named ride animation/controller configuration.
   These are outside the three currently patched entries. They were NOT
   enabled in this build because the confirmed current-scene blinking is fixed,
   and ride update cadence has not been measured. They are concrete future
   coverage targets if ride Pokemon still blink rapidly.
   Whole-text +68C/+644 load/decrement/store scan found those two plus the three
   already covered entries. Pattern matching is not proof of exhaustive coverage.

2. Script-frame producer identified:
   main+1050E00 reads clock channel 1 and converts nanoseconds to seconds at
   +1050E3C..+1050E54. At +1051860..+105187C, a context accumulates seconds in
   +84 but separately increments its integer +88 by exactly one.
   main+1059A70 returns the seconds; main+1059A78 returns the integer.
   WaitFrame update main+B35AE0 subtracts that integer from its +13C wait.
   Context flags can clear both fields at +105189C/+10518D4. This explains why
   a blanket replacement of the getter with floor(seconds*30) is unsafe: if the
   context resets every update, sub-frame fractions could be lost and waits
   could stall. A correction must retain remainder across the appropriate
   context lifetime. This is not yet identified as the NPC A/B advance guard.

3. Battle idle wait consumption verified:
   main+9BB904 initializes +238 from Trainer_IdleAnimationMin/MaxWaitFrames and
   IdleAnimationMinWaitFrames. main+9BAD68 checks +220 and subtracts one from
   +238 at +9BAD94, proceeding when <=0. This is an actual countdown, separate
   from eye blinking. Real call cadence remains unmeasured.

4. Cave/environment light blend confirmed:
   main+1E1B278 constructs 1/120, stores it as a blend increment, and
   main+1E1B2B0 adds it to progress on subsequent updates. Progress clamps to 1.
   Owner main+1E1A25C has environment/cave_light_change_easing names.
   If called once per render, 120 updates mean four seconds at 30 FPS and one
   second at 120 FPS. That is a conditional prediction, not an observed run.
   The denominator 120 is an authored update count, not necessarily an intended
   120-Hz simulation. Leave duration units intact when eventually correcting it.

5. Object motion writes verified:
   main+1D4892C adds 1/120 to +328 after writing an interpolated vector to the
   associated object's +D0 transform field and setting +E0.
   main+1D49838 adds 1/60 to the same timer after another transform update.
   Both are reached through main+1D48268. These are motion/transform updates,
   not harmless stand-alone constants, but the exact visible object/use and
   scheduler cadence remain unresolved.

6. Camera recentering remains separate:
   main+193633C increments +414 at +193644C and uses duration +45C, including
   reset_turn_keep_update_target logic. The stick response fix does not fix this
   integer-duration path. Test recentering separately from holding the stick.

7. Physics/navigation: instrumentation prepared, behavior unchanged:
   Navigation main+2795204 selects fixed 1/30 when +150 is zero; otherwise S0.
   Rigid-body main+2AF25B4 selects fixed 1/30 when +121 is zero; otherwise S0,
   with a separate +124 rate. Existing caller/constructor evidence is retained
   in arceus-timing-investigation.txt.
   SNX_ARCEUS_TIMING_RESEARCH now logs entry cadence, incoming dt, object, mode
   and rate. Enabled in the Responsive and Baseline launchers. It changes no
   timestep or physics state. These entry snapshots are not a trace inside the
   eventual solver. Fixed-step mode alone is not a bug if scheduled at 30 Hz.
   Compare flags AND calls/elapsed time before touching ball flight/physics.

SCAN LIMITATIONS

The strict scan found 5,180 candidates in 2,972 functions; only 34 had selected
timing words in the same function or direct callers. Neither number is a bug
count. Most matches are bookkeeping. Conversely, the scan misses timers with
branches between load/decrement/store, register aliases, vector clocks, fractional
steps, coroutine delays, or parameters with no nearby strings. Capture +1DC is a
concrete example of useful timing logic found by manual follow-up after a false
positive, rather than by the strict pattern itself.

throwable-progress states. Those findings and executable checks are documented
